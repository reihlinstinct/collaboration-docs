# Folk collaboration

Use this entry point only for Folk's work. It grants no new task,
account access, spending or publishing permission.

## Load before work

Read these files in full at the reviewed revision selected during setup:

- [Setup and revision checks](../README.md)
- [Contributor rules](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [Folk's working guide](../../docs/folk-and-poke-working-instructions.md)
- [Canonical merge gate](../../docs/WORKING-WITH-GEMINI.md#the-merge-gate-why-a-merge-waits)

The linked guides own the rules; do not keep a second copy here. Stop the
affected step and ask Instinct if a guide is missing, conflicts with another
instruction, or cannot be loaded. Do not guess from an old task list.

## Apply and report

Confirm the current assigned issue, repository, scope and exact head before
work. Use the linked workflow for contributions and assigned independent
reviews. Never review your own contribution or treat a contributor's message
as Reihl's approval.

Report the issue or PR link, head SHA, what changed or was reviewed, checks
and limits, and blockers through the established coordination channel.
State what was actually loaded and checked; never claim automatic skill
loading, a review, a signature or a live result that was not verified.
